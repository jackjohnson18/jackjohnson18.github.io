# Projects

My portfolio includes work in LiDAR processing, remote sensing, environmental modeling, water resources, machine learning, web GIS, and cartographic design.

## Featured Projects

### Endangered Species Suitability Modeling

Master’s thesis project using species occurrence records, climate data, terrain variables, soil information, and machine-learning methods to model current and future habitat suitability across Mexico.

[View project](thesis.md)

---

### Reproducible LiDAR Workflow Development

Development of a YAML-configured R pipeline that processes LiDAR and NAIP imagery into cleaned, masked, and deduplicated individual-tree crown polygons.

*Project page coming soon.*

---

### Tree Crown Delineation Using LiDAR and NAIP Imagery

Comparison of Dalponte, Silva, Li, and Watershed crown delineation methods against ground-truth tree crown polygons.

*Project page coming soon.*

### Antarctic Ocean Hotspots and Ice-Shelf Vulnerability

An ongoing project combining Sentinel imagery, elevation data, climate datasets, Google Earth Engine, Python, R, and machine learning to examine ice-shelf change.
