**Work as a graduate assistant researcher at California State University Long Beach in partnership with the Los Angeles Region Image Acquisition Consortium**

This is a series of models and analyses used to delineate tree crowns over Los Angeles County using LiDAR and NAIP Imagery. This is a project done at California State University Long Beach under the supervision of Dr. Jon Pando Ocon. This project is in collaboration with the Los Angeles Region Imagery Acquisition Consortium.

![](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRPWu1P-IMTGyy3SdpvC7wNIxr5EUFON4hFnqtKaj9Iw52ahPsy8LSKdaQ&s=10)

This workflow was done entirely in RStudio and written in the R language. We utilized 5 different crown delineation methods; all have been tested against one another as well as ground truth polygons collected in the winter of 2025 in the city of Long Beach.
