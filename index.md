# Jack Johnson

## GIS | Remote Sensing | Environmental Modeling | Spatial Data Science

I recently completed my Master of Science in Geographic Information Science at California State University, Long Beach. My work focuses on remote sensing, LiDAR, environmental modeling, spatial data science, and reproducible GIS workflows.

My research interests include urban forestry, habitat suitability modeling, water resources, Antarctic ice-shelf monitoring, climate-based prediction, and geospatial approaches to environmental change.

## Featured Projects

### Endangered Species Suitability Modeling

A reproducible spatial modeling workflow developed to predict endangered plant habitat suitability across Mexico using occurrence records, climate variables, terrain data, soil information, and machine-learning methods.

[View thesis project](thesis.md)

### Reproducible LiDAR Workflow Development

A configurable R pipeline that processes raw LiDAR and multispectral imagery to create normalized point clouds, canopy height models, and cleaned individual-tree crown polygons.

*Full project page coming soon.*

### Tree Crown Delineation Using LiDAR and NAIP Imagery

A comparison of Dalponte, Silva, Li, and Watershed tree-crown delineation methods using LiDAR, NAIP imagery, and ground-truth crown polygons.

*Full project page coming soon.*

### Antarctic Ocean Hotspots and Ice-Shelf Vulnerability

An ongoing remote-sensing project using satellite imagery, climate data, elevation models, and machine-learning workflows to examine Antarctic ice-shelf vulnerability.

*Full project page coming soon.*
